About The Daily Lens

Your focused daily news.

Who We Are

The Daily Lens is an online news publishing platform that brings readers politics, local, international, sports and education stories from Malawi and the wider world.

It was designed and built as a pair assignment for the Bachelor of Arts in Digital Journalism programme at the Malawi University of Business and Applied Sciences (MUBAS), using semantic HTML5, CSS and JavaScript.

What We Cover

  • Politics – government and parliament
  • Local News – community stories
  • International – world events
  • Sports – football and more
  • Education – schools and universities
